✦ Welcome ✦

About HAUS of Nishka

Hi loves! I'm Nishka — a passionate nail artist and nurse running a cozy, intimate home-based studio. I created HAUS of Nishka Studio as a space to grow my craft while giving my clients beautiful, affordable nails.

I only offer Gel X nails — my one true love in the nail world. Every set is done with care, creativity, and patience. Because I take my time to make sure everything is perfect, please allow up to 4 hours for your appointment.

🌸 Please Note: I am a beginner nail artist actively practicing and growing my skills. I charge only $30 per set — strictly to cover the cost of supplies. I appreciate your patience, your trust, and your support as I build my craft. Thank you for being part of my journey! 💗
